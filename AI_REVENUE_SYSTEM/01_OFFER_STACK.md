# OFFER STACK + PRICING LADDER

---

## CORE MICRO-OFFER TIERS

### TIER 1 — AI Listing/Service Launch Pack — $47
Entry offer. Low friction. Fast yes.

**Realtor version delivers:**
- MLS listing description (250–400 words, conversion-optimized)
- Instagram caption (with CTA)
- Facebook post (longer form, engagement-focused)
- Open house promo copy
- 15 niche hashtags
- 1 short Reel script (30–45 seconds)

**Mortgage version delivers:**
- First-time buyer promo copy
- 2 social captions (Instagram + Facebook)
- Short email nurture sequence (3 emails)
- CTA copy variations
- 5 lead magnet title ideas
- 1 Reel hook + video script

**Insurance version delivers:**
- 1 educational post (trust-building)
- Caption set (3 captions for different audiences)
- Follow-up email + SMS copy
- Objection-handling FAQ snippets (top 5 objections)
- Local promotion copy
- 2 short video hooks

---

### TIER 2 UPSELL 1 — Email + Text Follow-Up Pack — $37
Add-on after the core offer. Present immediately after sale.

**Delivers:**
- 3-email follow-up sequence (post-inquiry or post-open-house)
- 2 SMS follow-up scripts
- 1 re-engagement sequence (2 emails + 1 SMS)
- Subject line variations (A/B options)

**Pitch line:** "Want me to add the follow-up sequences so the leads you get from this content don't go cold? That's $37 more and I'll have it in the same delivery."

---

### TIER 3 UPSELL 2 — 7-Day Social Sprint — $97
Sell this as a content runway. Works especially well for realtors prepping for a busy listing period.

**Delivers:**
- 7 days of ready-to-post content
- Mix of: educational posts, listing promos, trust-builders, CTAs
- Platform-specific versions (Instagram, Facebook)
- Caption variations for each post
- 7 hashtag sets
- 3 Reel hook options

**Pitch line:** "If you want a full week of content loaded and ready so you're not scrambling every morning, I can add a 7-Day Social Sprint for $97. Everything's formatted and ready to drop in."

---

### FULL BUNDLE — Full Visibility Bundle — $147
Present this as the anchor offer. Position the individual tiers as "parts of the bundle."

**Delivers everything in Tier 1 + Upsell 1 + partial Upsell 2:**
- Complete listing or service launch content
- Follow-up email + SMS sequences
- 5-day social content calendar (condensed from 7-day)
- Reel scripts + hooks
- Hashtag sets
- CTA variations
- One round of edits included

**Positioning:** "The Full Visibility Bundle is everything you need to launch a listing [or service offer], follow up with leads, and stay active on social for the next week. All done-for-you. $147."

## OBJECTION HANDLING AT POINT OF SALE

**"I don't have budget right now."**
→ "No problem — I can do just the launch pack for $47 to get one listing done. If you like it, the bundle's there when the next listing hits."

**"I already have someone who does this."**
→ "Got it. Out of curiosity, are they turning it around same-day? I deliver everything within 2–4 hours. If you ever need fast backup coverage, keep me in mind."

**"I need to think about it."**
→ "Totally. Let me send you a free sample for [their active listing/service]. No strings. If it's not better than what you have, you don't owe me anything."

**"What makes this different from ChatGPT?"**
→ "The prompts are built specifically for [realtors/lenders/insurance]. The output is formatted, edited, and localized — not raw AI. You paste and post. That's the difference."

## UPSELL TIMING

1. Close the core offer ($47 or $147)
2. Immediately after confirmation: "One more thing — want me to add the follow-up sequences? That's $37 and it goes in the same delivery."
3. After delivery: "How'd it perform? If you want a full week loaded, the 7-Day Sprint is $97 and I can start on it today."

Never present all three tiers at once. Stack them after each yes.
