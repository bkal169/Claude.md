## DEMAND FUSION: WHERE TO FIND TARGETS

### Realtor Targets
- Instagram: search "[city] realtor", "[city] homes for sale" — look for accounts with <5K followers, inconsistent posting, weak captions, no Reels
- Facebook: search "[city] real estate agent" — check their business page for last post date
- Zillow/Realtor.com: find active listings with thin descriptions (under 100 words, no lifestyle language)
- Google Business: search "realtor [city]" — check for no posts, few reviews, stale photos
- Facebook Groups: "Real Estate Agents [state]" — look for people asking for content help or complaining about marketing

### Mortgage Broker Targets
- LinkedIn: search "mortgage broker [city]" — look for sparse activity
- Instagram: "[city] mortgage" — weak follower counts, no Reels, generic posts
- Upwork: search "mortgage content", "real estate copywriting" — active job posts = active demand

### Insurance Agent Targets
- Instagram: "[city] insurance agent" — educational content is usually missing or robotic
- Facebook: business pages with >6 months since last post
- Google: "State Farm agent [city]" — independent agents especially need content help

### Demand Signals to Look For
- Last post was 2+ weeks ago
- Captions are generic ("Just listed! DM for details")
- No Reels in the last 30 days
- Listing descriptions are thin and feature-only (no lifestyle, no emotion)
- No follow-up sequence visible in their funnel
- Inconsistent hashtag use or no hashtags
- Bio doesn't have a clear CTA

**Weak execution = visible demand. Treat every stale account as a buyer.**

## TARGETING WORKFLOW (Silent Distribution Engine)

```
STEP 1: Research (30 min)
  → Find 20 targets with visible gaps
  → Note: their name, platform, specific gap, active listing or product

STEP 2: Personalize (15 min)
  → Fill in [Name], [specific listing/product], [platform], [gap]
  → Do NOT send generic blasts

STEP 3: Send (15 min)
  → Instagram DMs: 10 targets
  → Facebook Messenger: 5 targets
  → Email: 5 targets

STEP 4: Follow up (next morning)
  → Send Follow-Up 1 to all non-responders

STEP 5: Respond and close (same day as reply)
  → Offer free sample within 2 hours
  → Deliver sample using fulfillment prompts
  → Close on $47 or $147

STEP 6: Upsell
  → After payment confirmation, trigger upsell script
  → Stack toward $147+ per client
```
