# EXECUTION PLANS
## 24-Hour Sprint + 72-Hour Optimization

---

## 24-HOUR EXECUTION PLAN
### Goal: First conversation started, first sample delivered, first sale possible

---

### HOUR 0–2: SETUP (Do this once, then never again)

**Accounts to have ready:**
- [ ] Gmail or email address for outreach (use your name or a simple business name — nothing fancy)
- [ ] Instagram account (personal or basic business, doesn't need followers)
- [ ] Facebook personal or page (to DM and join groups)
- [ ] Google Doc template ready (copy the delivery template from `03_FULFILLMENT_PROMPTS.md`)
- [ ] Payment method live: Venmo, CashApp, PayPal, or Stripe (all free to set up)
- [ ] Claude.ai or ChatGPT Plus open and ready

**Offer clarity:**
- [ ] Memorize or paste the offer stack (from `01_OFFER_STACK.md`)
- [ ] Know your three numbers cold: $47, $97, $147
- [ ] Know your upsell trigger phrase cold

**That's it. No website needed. No portfolio needed. The sample IS the portfolio.**

---

### HOUR 2–4: TARGET IDENTIFICATION (Demand Fusion)

Search Instagram, Facebook, and Zillow for 20–25 realtor targets in ONE city.

**Target criteria (need at least 2 of these):**
- Last post was 7+ days ago
- Captions are generic ("Just listed! Call me!")
- No Reels in the last 30 days
- Active listings on Zillow with weak descriptions (under 150 words, no lifestyle language)
- Bio has no clear CTA or has a dead link
- Under 1,000 followers with active listings (they're doing real business but not marketing well)

**Build a simple tracking list (Google Sheet or Notes app):**
| Name | Platform | Profile Link | Gap Identified | Listing/Service | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jane Smith | Instagram | @janesmith_homes | No Reels, stale captions | 123 Main St | Not contacted |

Collect 20–25 names before you send a single DM.

---

### HOUR 4–6: OUTREACH WAVE 1

Send 10 personalized DMs. Use Instagram and Facebook Messenger for speed.

**Rules:**
- Personalize every single one (name + specific gap + their specific listing)
- Do NOT copy-paste the same message to everyone on the same platform (shadow ban risk)
- Space them out slightly — don't blast 10 in 5 minutes
- Reference their actual listing address or their actual account by name

**Variants to rotate through (from `02_OUTREACH_SCRIPTS.md`):**
- Master DM (base)
- Instagram DM (short version)
- Facebook Messenger (warmer version)

Target: 10 DMs sent in this window.

---

### HOUR 6–8: FIRST RESPONSES / SAMPLE DELIVERY

If anyone responds — even just "what is this?" — treat it as a warm lead.

**Response protocol:**
1. Acknowledge quickly (within 30 minutes)
2. Offer the free sample: "I can put together a quick sample for [their listing] right now — just so you can see exactly what you'd be getting. Takes me about an hour."
3. Ask one qualifying question: "What's your next listing?" or "What's your current focus — buyers, listings, or both?"

**Building the free sample:**
- Use the "Sample Free Deliverable Prompt" from `03_FULFILLMENT_PROMPTS.md`
- Get their listing info from Zillow if they haven't sent it yet
- Run the prompt, edit the output, paste into a Google Doc
- Name the doc: "Sample Content — [Their Name] — [Listing Address]"
- Share with view access via Google Doc link

**Target: 1 sample delivered in this window.**

---

### HOUR 8–12: CLOSE AND UPSELL

After delivering the sample:

1. **Wait 1–2 hours** (don't follow up immediately — let them read it)
2. Send the close message:

```
Hey [Name] — sent that over. Everything's labeled and ready to use as-is.

If you want the full pack — all the social content, follow-up sequences, and hashtag sets — it's $47 for the launch pack or $147 for the full visibility bundle including a week of content and follow-up email/SMS scripts.

Which one fits better?
```

3. If they say yes to either: send the payment link immediately (Venmo, CashApp, PayPal)
4. Build the full deliverable using the master fulfillment prompt
5. Deliver in Google Doc within 2–4 hours
6. Trigger the upsell: "Want me to add the follow-up email + SMS sequences? That's $37 — I'll add it to the same doc."

---

### HOUR 12–16: OUTREACH WAVE 2

While waiting for responses from Wave 1:
- Send 8–10 more DMs
- Add 5 cold emails to agents with visible email addresses (from their website or Zillow profile)
- Post 1–2 Upwork proposals if relevant job posts exist

---

### HOUR 16–24: FOLLOW-UP + REVIEW

- Follow up on Wave 1 non-responses (use Follow-Up 1 script)
- Respond to any new DM replies immediately
- Complete any pending deliverables
- Track: DMs sent / responses / samples delivered / sales

**End of Day 1 Target:**
- 20 DMs sent
- 2–4 responses
- 1–2 samples delivered
- 1 sale (stretch: 2 sales)

---

## 72-HOUR OPTIMIZATION PLAN
### Goal: $330+ closed, system tuned, second wave ready

---

### DAY 2 (Hours 24–48): FOLLOW-UP AND CLOSE

**Morning (first 2 hours):**
- Check all DM inboxes for overnight responses
- Send Follow-Up 1 to all Day 1 non-responses
- Close any open sample-delivered conversations

**Midday:**
- Send Wave 3 outreach (10 more targets — now expand to mortgage brokers)
- Deliver any pending paid orders from Day 1
- Upsell delivered clients into follow-up pack

**Afternoon:**
- Review what's working in your DMs: which opener got the best response rate?
- Adjust the lowest-performing variant
- Note any friction in the close — what objections came up?

**Day 2 Target:**
- $330+ closed (two $147 bundles + one upsell, or equivalent)
- Follow-up system running

---

### DAY 3 (Hours 48–72): OPTIMIZE AND STACK

**Morning:**
- Identify your best-converting message variant from Days 1–2
- Make it your new "base" script
- Send 15 more outreach messages using the optimized version

**Midday:**
- Build a Google Doc "sample library" — save your best 3 samples as reusable templates
- Create a light portfolio: screenshots or links to 2–3 sample docs (anonymized or with permission)
- Add this portfolio as a P.S. to future outreach: "Here's a sample I built for another agent: [link]"

**Afternoon:**
- Identify your most common question or objection from outreach
- Build a one-sentence answer to it and add it to your DM script
- Draft a 3-question intake form (Google Form) to speed up client onboarding:
  1. Business type (realtor / mortgage / insurance)
  2. Current listing or service focus
  3. Target audience and tone preference

**Day 3 Target:**
- 40–50 total DMs sent across all 3 days
- 3–5 sales completed
- $300–$500 in total revenue
- Clear winner variant for outreach script
- Sample library built with 3 reusable templates

**If response rate is good but close rate is low:** Your sample isn't converting. Make the sample bigger/better.
**If close rate is good but units are low:** You need more outreach volume. Double the DM count.

## DECISION TREE: What to Do When

```
Got a response but they're cold?
  → Offer the free sample immediately. Don't pitch yet.

They love the sample but say they'll "think about it"?
  → "Totally — what's your next listing? I'll hold the rate."

They ask about price first before seeing anything?
  → "Let me send you a sample first — then you'll know if it's worth it."

They ghost after the sample?
  → Wait 24 hours, then send Follow-Up 3 (the "did it land?" check-in)

They say they don't have budget?
  → Drop to $47 entry. If still no, park them and move on.

They want revisions after delivery?
  → Do one round free. Second round is a paid add-on or builds goodwill for renewal.

They want to share with their team or office?
  → That's a team/brokerage deal. Offer a multi-license pack: 3 agents for $297/month.
```
