# FULFILLMENT PROMPTS
## Master AI Prompt System for All Three Verticals

---

## HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS

1. Copy the master prompt for the relevant vertical
2. Fill in the bracketed fields with client-specific info
3. Run it in Claude (claude.ai), ChatGPT-4, or your preferred model
4. Review output — edit for local feel, accuracy, and tone
5. Paste into a Google Doc formatted per the delivery template
6. Send to client

Turnaround target: **2–4 hours from payment to delivery.**

## MASTER FULFILLMENT PROMPT — REALTOR VERSION

```
You are an expert real estate copywriter who specializes in high-converting listing content and social media for residential real estate agents.

Create a complete AI Listing Launch Pack for the following property:

BUSINESS TYPE: Real estate agent / residential realtor
LISTING DETAILS:
- Address: [full address or neighborhood]
- Price: [$X]
- Bedrooms/Bathrooms: [X bed / X bath]
- Square footage: [X sqft]
- Key features: [list 5–8 features: e.g., renovated kitchen, pool, open floor plan, etc.]
- Neighborhood highlights: [e.g., top-rated schools, walkable to downtown, quiet cul-de-sac]
- Listing status: [active / coming soon / just listed / open house on DATE]

TARGET AUDIENCE: [First-time buyers / move-up buyers / investors / luxury buyers / downsizers]
TONE: [Professional and warm / Luxury and aspirational / Approachable and friendly / Urgency-focused]
GOAL: [Drive showings / Drive open house attendance / Generate inquiry calls / Build social engagement]
AGENT NAME: [Agent's name]
MARKET/CITY: [City, State]

OUTPUT REQUIRED — create all of the following:

1. SHORT HEADLINE (10 words max, emotionally compelling)
2. MLS LISTING DESCRIPTION (250–350 words, lifestyle-forward, feature-rich, ends with CTA)
3. INSTAGRAM CAPTION (150–200 characters, conversational, ends with question or CTA, emoji where natural)
4. FACEBOOK POST (200–300 words, more narrative, includes key features, ends with CTA)
5. OPEN HOUSE PROMO COPY (short post format, includes date/time placeholder, urgency)
6. 3 REEL HOOKS (each under 10 words, pattern-interrupt style, designed to stop the scroll)
7. 1 SHORT REEL SCRIPT (30–45 seconds, voiceover style, includes hook, 3 features, CTA)
8. 2 EMAIL SUBJECT LINES (A/B options, curiosity or benefit-driven)
9. 1 SHORT EMAIL (under 200 words, works as a listing announcement to sphere of influence list)
10. 2 SMS FOLLOW-UPS (under 160 characters each, post-inquiry or post-open-house)
11. 15 HASHTAGS (mix of local, niche, and broad — optimized for Instagram reach)
12. 3 CTA VARIATIONS (one soft, one direct, one urgency)
13. URGENCY LINE (if open house or limited-time positioning applies)

Format each section with a clear header. Write in a way that feels local and human — not robotic or templated.
```

## MASTER FULFILLMENT PROMPT — MORTGAGE BROKER VERSION

```
You are an expert financial services copywriter who specializes in mortgage and lending content for social media, email, and lead generation.

Create a complete AI Service Launch Pack for the following mortgage professional:

BUSINESS TYPE: Mortgage broker / loan officer / lending company
SERVICE DETAILS:
- Primary loan products offered: [e.g., FHA, conventional, VA, USDA, jumbo, HELOC]
- Current promotion or focus: [e.g., first-time buyer campaign, low-rate refi, down payment assistance program]
- Key differentiators: [e.g., fast closings, local lender, in-house underwriting, bilingual service]
- Service area: [City, State or region]

TARGET AUDIENCE: [First-time buyers / Repeat buyers / Veterans / Self-employed borrowers / Move-up buyers]
TONE: [Trustworthy and educational / Warm and approachable / Urgent and opportunity-driven / Professional]
GOAL: [Generate inbound leads / Build trust / Promote a specific product / Drive consultation bookings]
BROKER/OFFICER NAME: [Name]
COMPANY NAME: [Company, if applicable]

OUTPUT REQUIRED — create all of the following:

1. SHORT HEADLINE (10 words max, benefit-driven)
2. FIRST-TIME BUYER PROMO COPY (200–300 words, educational but motivating, ends with CTA)
3. INSTAGRAM CAPTION (conversational, benefit-forward, CTA at end)
4. FACEBOOK POST (longer narrative, addresses a common buyer concern, ends with CTA)
5. 3-EMAIL NURTURE SEQUENCE:
   - Email 1: Introduction / value-first (subject line + body)
   - Email 2: Education / myth-busting (subject line + body)
   - Email 3: CTA / next step (subject line + body)
6. 3 REEL HOOKS (scroll-stopping, under 10 words each)
7. 1 SHORT REEL SCRIPT (30–45 seconds, addresses one specific buyer concern or myth)
8. 5 LEAD MAGNET TITLE IDEAS (downloadable guide concepts for first-time buyers or refi prospects)
9. 3 CTA VARIATIONS (consultation booking focused)
10. 2 SMS FOLLOW-UPS (post-lead or post-consultation inquiry)
11. 15 HASHTAGS (lending, home buying, local market focused)
12. OBJECTION-BUSTING ONE-LINER (for the most common objection in their target audience)

Format each section clearly. Keep language compliance-aware — avoid rate promises or guarantees. Focus on process, education, and outcomes.
```

## MASTER FULFILLMENT PROMPT — INSURANCE AGENT VERSION

```
You are an expert insurance content strategist and copywriter who creates trust-building, educational social content for independent and captive insurance agents.

Create a complete AI Service Launch Pack for the following insurance professional:

BUSINESS TYPE: Insurance agent (specify: [independent / State Farm / Allstate / Farmers / other])
PRIMARY PRODUCTS: [e.g., auto, home, life, umbrella, business, renters — list their focus]
CURRENT CAMPAIGN OR FOCUS: [e.g., home protection awareness, life insurance month, local event tie-in, seasonal auto]
KEY DIFFERENTIATORS: [e.g., local office, family-owned, multilingual, fast claims support, 24-hr service]
SERVICE AREA: [City, State or region]

TARGET AUDIENCE: [Homeowners / Families / Young professionals / Small business owners / Seniors]
TONE: [Trustworthy and educational / Community-focused / Warm and reassuring / Straightforward and clear]
GOAL: [Build credibility / Generate referrals / Drive policy reviews / Increase local awareness]
AGENT NAME: [Name]

OUTPUT REQUIRED — create all of the following:

1. SHORT HEADLINE (10 words max, trust or protection-themed)
2. EDUCATIONAL POST (250–300 words, teaches something useful about their primary product, builds trust, not salesy)
3. TRUST-BUILDING CAPTION SET (3 captions: one for a community story, one for a client win [anonymized], one for an educational tip)
4. FOLLOW-UP EMAIL (post-quote or post-inquiry, under 200 words, warm and low-pressure)
5. 2 SMS FOLLOW-UPS (post-quote or post-referral, under 160 characters each)
6. OBJECTION-HANDLING FAQ SNIPPETS (top 5 objections with short, natural-sounding responses)
   - "I already have insurance through work"
   - "It's too expensive"
   - "I'll think about it"
   - "I don't need that much coverage"
   - "I've never had a claim so I don't need it"
7. LOCAL PROMOTION COPY (ties the agent to a local event, season, or community moment)
8. 2 SHORT VIDEO HOOKS (education angle and personal/trust angle)
9. 3 REEL HOOKS (pattern-interrupt, curiosity-based)
10. 2 EMAIL SUBJECT LINES (for the follow-up email above)
11. 15 HASHTAGS (local, insurance-specific, community-focused)
12. 3 CTA VARIATIONS (policy review, free quote, referral ask)

Keep all content human, local-feeling, and trust-first. Avoid insurance jargon. Write as if the agent is talking to a neighbor.
```
